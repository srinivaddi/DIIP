DIIP Stock Scoring Rubric

To calculate opportunity ranking, score each stock ticker from 0 to 100 based on the following framework:

1. Thematic Pure-Play Exposure (Up to 40 points):
   - 40 pts: Company's revenue is >80% driven by the theme.
   - 25 pts: Company is a key value-chain enabler.
   - 10 pts: Secondary/derivative exposure.

2. Macro Alignment (Up to 20 points):
   - 20 pts: Positive exposure to current Macro regime (e.g. high interest rates benefit financial/value stocks).
   - 10 pts: Neutral exposure.

3. Flow Sentiment (Up to 20 points):
   - 20 pts: Strong net positive ETF and institutional inflows over the past 30 days.
   - 0 pts: Negative/heavy outflows.

4. Financial / Valuation Health (Up to 20 points):
   - 20 pts: Strong earnings growth, stable/widening margins, and trading below historical P/E median.
